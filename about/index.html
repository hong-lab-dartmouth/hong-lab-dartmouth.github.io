About

What we do

The Hong Lab studies neurological disease through a combination of clinical research, computational analysis, and surgical innovation. Our work focuses on improving patient outcomes by developing tools that help clinicians understand, monitor, and treat disorders of the brain, spine, and peripheral nerves.

Clinical Neuroscience

We investigate the mechanisms underlying neurological dysfunction in patients with brain, spinal, and peripheral nerve disorders. Our research integrates surgical experience with longitudinal clinical data to better understand recovery, risk factors, and treatment response.

Technology for Medicine

The lab develops computational tools that support clinical decision making. This includes imaging analysis pipelines, outcome prediction models, and data systems that enable reliable tracking of neurological function over time.

Imaging and Data Science

We apply modern machine learning and statistical approaches to MRI, electrophysiology, and clinical datasets. Our goal is to extract meaningful signals that can guide diagnosis, monitor disease progression, and inform surgical planning.

Surgical Innovation

Research in the lab directly informs operative techniques and perioperative care. We evaluate neuromonitoring strategies, surgical workflows, and postoperative recovery patterns to improve safety and long term neurological outcomes.

Translational Focus

Our work is designed to move from research into practice. We prioritize projects that can be deployed in real clinical environments, including hospital systems, surgical settings, and rehabilitation programs.

Training and Collaboration

The Hong Lab trains medical students, graduate students, and undergraduate researchers in interdisciplinary methods spanning neuroscience, engineering, and clinical medicine. We collaborate closely with clinicians, scientists, and technologists to translate discoveries into patient care.
